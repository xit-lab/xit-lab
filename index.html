The Next-Generation Information Technology (xIT) Lab. at the University of Ulsan researches error correction codes, AI-driven communication systems, quantum error correction, and reliable information technologies for robust next-generation systems.

xIT Lab에서는 ECC, AI, 양자 오류 정정, LLM 워터마킹과 같은 기술을 바탕으로 통신 시스템, 반도체, DNA 저장장치, 양자 컴퓨팅의 reliability와 performance를 높이는 연구를 수행합니다.

Research Areas

Neural ECC Decoder

Transformer-based and neural message-passing decoders that improve reliability for modern error-correcting codes.

RL ECC Construction

Reinforcement learning methods for discovering strong code structures under practical communication constraints.

Quantum Error Correction

Coding and decoding strategies for protecting quantum information against noise and decoherence.

LLM Text Watermark

Robust text watermarking techniques that support responsible deployment and provenance tracking of language models.

AI Image Detection

Learning-based detectors for identifying AI-generated images and protecting media authenticity.
